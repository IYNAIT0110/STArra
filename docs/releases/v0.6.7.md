# STArra v0.6.7

## 中文

- 外观提供八个主题。六个磨砂主题支持一键开启或关闭固定强度的桌面背景模糊，另有两套黑白实体主题。
- 纵向布局的功能栏固定在左、详情在右。展开的面板更大，独立侧栏与展开后的侧栏等高。
- 侧栏贴边时，功能面板随侧栏滑动收起。悬停主题色手柄可恢复刚才打开的页面和编辑状态。
- 传送门按可用空间调整每页行列，保持网站 Logo 尺寸。
- 星砂的「使用文档」新增 v0.6.7 功能介绍。

Windows 10 / 11 x64。下载 `starra-0.6.7-setup.exe` 安装，或使用 `starra-0.6.7-portable.exe`。可用 `SHA256SUMS.txt` 核对文件。

## English

- Choose from eight themes, including six frosted themes with a desktop blur toggle and two solid monochrome themes.
- In vertical layout, navigation stays on the left and details open on the right. The workspace is larger, and the sidebar keeps the same height when opened on its own.
- The workspace slides away with the docked sidebar. Hover over the theme-colored edge handle to restore the previous page and editing state.
- Portal fits more websites per page when there is room and keeps their logo size.
- Star Sand includes a v0.6.7 guide in its built-in documentation folder.

For Windows 10 / 11 x64, use the setup or portable executable. Verify either download against `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
